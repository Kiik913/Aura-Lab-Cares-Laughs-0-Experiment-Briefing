Aura Lab: Cares & Laughs 0 — Experiment Briefing
🧪
What this is
Tiny lab experiment where your brain farms $VG (Vibes Generated), XP, and hearts while the game quietly turns more evil.
  • Clicker core → generate vibes.
  • Auto‑difficulty from easy to hard.
  • Hidden Level 100 “game died” ending.
⚙️
Core systems
Everything you do feeds a few main systems that decide how nice or cruel Aura Lab feels.
  • Vibes & $VG: each good click shows a random line and gives $VG. Some vibe milestones drop bonuses.
  • Levels & XP: XP rules get weirder as difficulty rises; level up → bigger XP bar and meaner game.
  • Timer & hearts: each level has a countdown. Hit 0 or go AFK too long = lose a heart.
🛒
Upgrades shop
Spend $VG to bend the rules instead of suffering them.
  • Extra Hearts: more lives (up to 6), price climbs each time.
  • Time Bender: adds seconds to the level timer.
  • Brain Overclock: extra XP per click, stacks up to +3.
  • Background Vibes: passive $VG per second.
  • Heart Shield: blocks the next heart loss.
  • Pro Aura Lab: 600 $VG flex; timer + XP buff and PRO title.
🕵️‍♂️
Secrets & progress
Aura Lab remembers more than you think.
  • Rage Quit: unlocks all external rewards once per tab, then gets permanently disabled.
  • Secret code: certain keyboard sequences (like “AURA”) can instantly unlock everything.
  • Level 100: triggers a glitchy ending and credits screen.
  • Runs: Reset or Game Over wipes the run but your total $VG in this tab stays.
When you’re ready, hit “Start the experiment” and see how far your hearts, timer, and patience go before everything breaks.
